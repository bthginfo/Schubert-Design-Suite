## Objective and users
German-speaking Schubert office/practical employees, including people without much digital experience, must select or search any dropdown; manipulate memorial/room plans easily; and understand controls without memorizing icons. The user requests noticeably more spatial separation between content/background (subtle quiet glow, Apple-like clarity) and a planning editor inspired by Figma, with true fullscreen. Do not copy logos/assets or mimic an unrelated product at the expense of craft workflows.

## Visual direction
